This page is a supplement to my Flight Perspective Generator to demonstrate the amount of global heating produced by various celebrities. I used data on celebrity private flights from the Celebrity Private Jet Tracker1 taken on , which provides:

  • total miles flown (which I convert to kilometres)
  • number of flights taken so far
  • gallons of fuel used (I assume US gallons of Jet A, which I convert to kilograms)
  • metric tons of CO2 generated (which I convert to kilograms)
From these values I have derived data for each average flight: the 'burn' (kilograms burned per kilometre travelled), the average kilometres per flight, the total CO2 pollution including that released during mining, drilling, refining, and transporting the fuel, the amount of heat generated, and from that the number of Hiroshima-equivalents being generated perpetually from the resulting greenhouse gases.
